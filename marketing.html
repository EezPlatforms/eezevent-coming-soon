Organize inquiries, follow up with clients, and track opportunities from new and pending to confirmed. Pro adds local event leads and outreach.

Choose your workspace

Basic or Pro, built around your business

Clients can plan events without a vendor subscription. Vendor owners choose the plan that fits their business.

Basic

For one active business.

  • Business profile, multiple services, and service catalog
  • Client inquiries and messaging
  • Calendar and availability tools

Pro

For up to three owned businesses.

  • Everything in Basic
  • Business and service team access
  • Priority placement in relevant searches
  • Local event leads and outreach for eligible events

Eligible subscribers can receive a six-month introductory free trial. Review current pricing, trial eligibility, and renewal terms in the app's Apple or Google purchase screen. Subscriptions renew monthly unless canceled through the store. Team members do not need a separate subscription to access an eligible owner's Pro business.

Pro outreach respects client outreach preferences. Priority placement is identified as Promoted and does not guarantee inquiries or bookings. See the Vendor Agreement for details.
