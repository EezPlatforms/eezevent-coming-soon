A few helpful answers

Before you get started.

Who is EezEvent for?

EezEvent helps event vendors such as DJs, caterers, photographers, decorators, and venues manage their business. Clients and planners use the same app to organize events and connect with vendors.

Do clients need a paid vendor plan?

No. Vendor owners choose Basic or Pro. Clients can create events and connect with vendors without a vendor subscription.

Can my team work with me?

Business and service team access is included with an eligible owner's Pro business. Those team members do not need a separate subscription to work in that business.

Where do quotes, contracts, and other files go?

Keep supporting documents with the relevant inquiry so the files remain connected to the booking discussion. Your calendar helps you review upcoming commitments.

How does the introductory trial work?

Eligible subscribers may receive a six-month introductory trial. The app's purchasing store shows eligibility, price, billing period, and renewal terms before purchase. Subscriptions renew monthly unless canceled through the store.

Where can I get help?

Visit the support and setup guide or contact support@eezevent.com.
