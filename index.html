HOW EEZEVENT WORKS

From getting started
to getting it organized.

Download EezEvent, choose the vendor or client path, and sign in. Then follow the workflow that fits your role.

Get the app ↓
FOR EVENT BUSINESSES

Turn inquiries into organized work.

  1. Set up your business and services

    Review Basic and Pro options, create your business, and add service details, photos, pricing, and a catalog so clients can explore your offerings.

  2. Review inquiries and send quotes

    Open a client's inquiry to see the event details. Ask questions in Messages and share a quote with the next steps.

  3. Agree on the work

    When the client accepts a quote, issue a contract and follow its signing status. Keep the booking discussion and documents with the inquiry.

  4. Keep delivery on track

    Review confirmed work, calendar commitments, and items needing attention from your business workspace.

Business setup guide →
FOR CLIENTS & HOSTS

Bring your event plans together.

  1. Create your event

    Add the event type, location, date, guest count, budget, and services you need to give your planning a starting point.

  2. Find vendors and send inquiries

    Search by service type, explore profiles and photos, and send vendors an inquiry with your event and a message. Confirm availability directly with each vendor.

  3. Review quotes and confirm details

    Discuss options in Messages, accept a suitable quote, and review and sign the vendor's contract when you're ready to proceed.

  4. Coordinate the celebration

    Keep vendor conversations and documents together. Add your invitation image, share the RSVP link, and track guest responses.
