# EezEvent Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 21, 2026

**Version:** 2026-10-21

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how **EezPlatforms LLC** ("EezEvent," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you access or use the EezEvent mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that we collect and use information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, do not use the Platform.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of the Platform, and limited technical information needed to operate the Platform.

### 1.1 Account and Authentication Information

We may collect:

* Name, email address, and phone number.
* Account role, such as client, event planner, vendor owner, business team member, service team member, or event team member.
* Planner status and account preferences.
* Login credentials when email/password login is available.
* Information received from authentication providers, including Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In, such as identity tokens and account profile information needed to create or authenticate your EezEvent account.
* Legal document acceptance records, including accepted policy or agreement version and acceptance timestamp.

### 1.2 Client, Planner, and Event Information

Clients and planners may provide:

* Event names, descriptions, event types, dates, start and end times, zip codes, guest counts, budgets, and requested service categories.
* Event profile images and other uploaded event materials.
* Client contact information used by planners or vendors to create or manage events on behalf of a client.
* Event team member details, including names, email addresses, and phone numbers.

### 1.3 Vendor, Business, and Service Information

Vendors and vendor team members may provide:

* Business names, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, descriptions, and business addresses.
* Service information, including service type, service area, pricing, service descriptions, social links, availability details, and related business information.
* Service images, main photos, catalog item images, catalog titles, catalog prices, catalog notes, and display order.
* Business team member and service team member details, including names, email addresses, and phone numbers.
* Calendar blocks, unavailable time periods, reservations, and scheduling details.

### 1.4 Inquiry, Messaging, Quote, Contract, and Document Information

When users interact through the Platform, we may collect:

* Vendor search, outreach, and lead information.
* Inquiry details, inquiry status, quote amounts, quote responses, contract status, scheduling status, cancellation status, completion status, and inquiry audit history.
* Messages and conversation metadata between clients, planners, vendors, and team members.
* Uploaded inquiry attachments, such as quotes, contracts, invoices, schedules, permits, receipts, and other supporting documents.
* Availability checks and overlapping commitment information used to help users understand scheduling conflicts.

### 1.5 Photos, Files, and Device Permissions

The mobile app may request access to your photo library so you can upload event images, vendor service images, catalog item images, and inquiry attachments. We collect the files you choose to upload. We do not access photos or files unless you select them for use with the Platform.

The permissions and file-selection options offered depend on your device and app version. You can decline optional permissions, although the corresponding upload feature may be unavailable. Store-billed subscriptions are described in Section 3.

### 1.6 Notifications and Device Information

If you enable or interact with push notifications, we may collect:

* Expo push notification token.
* Device operating system.
* Device label or device name when available.
* Notification-related metadata, such as unread message counts or inquiry identifiers needed to route you to the right screen.

We may also collect device and technical information such as IP address, browser type when using web pages, device type, timestamps, app version, update channel, runtime version, logs, diagnostics, and server request information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and update the Platform.

### 1.7 Local Storage and Session Information

The mobile app may store limited session and preference information on your device, such as authentication token, user type, email address, phone number, name, planner status, theme preference, and cached feature state. Website pages may use browser storage or cookies for session or site functionality if needed.

### 1.8 Information from Other Users and Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

* Clients, planners, vendors, business team members, service team members, and event team members who invite you, message you, create an event involving you, or add your contact details to a team or workflow.
* Google and Apple when you use their sign-in services.
* Service providers that help us operate the Platform.

### 1.9 Subscription and Purchase Information

For Basic and Pro subscriptions, we collect information linked to your EezEvent account, including plan, store provider, product identifier, transaction and order identifiers, original transaction identifiers or purchase tokens, trial and billing dates, renewal status, pending plan changes, and store verification responses. These records allow us to verify purchases and determine subscription access.

### 1.10 Ratings, Reviews, and Responses

When you submit or edit a review, we collect its rating, comments, creation and update times, and links to your account and the relevant inquiry, service, and business. When a business owner responds, we collect the response and its update time. We use these records to check review eligibility, prevent duplicate reviews, calculate service rating summaries, display reviews and responses, and address support or abuse reports. We may notify the relevant business owner of a new review.

### 1.11 Location and Service-Area Information

We collect event and business addresses or ZIP codes you provide, service-area ZIP codes and travel radii, and associated location coordinates supplied through Platform workflows or derived from ZIP reference data. We use these details to match service coverage with event locations, calculate approximate distances, and support vendor search and outreach. ZIP-derived coordinates describe an approximate area, not an exact street location. The current discovery flow uses saved or entered location information rather than requiring live device GPS access.

## 4. How We Share Information

Paid Pro subscriptions include event discovery and outreach as described in Section 4.1.1, subject to your event outreach settings.

We may share information in the following ways:

### 4.1 With Clients, Planners, Vendors, and Team Members

Information may be shared with other users when needed to provide Platform features. For example:

* Event details, service needs, messages, inquiries, uploaded documents, quote status, contract status, scheduling information, and contact information may be visible to users involved in the relevant event, inquiry, business, service, or team workflow.
* Vendor business profiles, service details, pricing, photos, catalog items, public contact information, and availability-related information may be shown to clients and planners.
* Client or planner event information may be shown to vendors when a user sends an inquiry, responds to outreach, or otherwise interacts with a vendor.

### 4.1.1 Pro Lead Discovery and Outreach

Eligible Pro vendors and their authorized team members can discover relevant event opportunities and send outreach through the Platform as part of their paid subscription. Event information used for matching and outreach may include event descriptions, service needs, ZIP code, dates, guest count, and budget. This discovery can occur before you send an inquiry or respond to a vendor.

In the outreach workflow, we withhold your client name, email address, and phone number from the vendor until you respond to that vendor's outreach. After your response, those details may be shared with that vendor and its authorized team members to coordinate services. Avoid including contact information in event descriptions or other materials visible during discovery if you do not want to disclose it there.

You can opt out of vendor lead discovery and unsolicited outreach for an event when creating the event or by changing the setting on an existing event. Opting out hides that event from Pro lead discovery and prevents new unsolicited outreach for that event. You are not required to respond to outreach or book a vendor. This event-level control does not limit privacy rights available under applicable law; you may contact us as described in Section 7 to exercise those rights.

### 4.1.2 Reviews and Public Responses

Review ratings, written comments, creation dates, and reviewer display names are visible to people who can view the relevant service profile, together with business-owner responses and response update dates. The current review display uses the reviewer's first name and last-name initial when available; these details can still identify you. We also display service rating averages and review counts. Your account and inquiry identifiers are used to associate reviews with the correct service and enforce eligibility; your written review and the displayed name are not anonymous.

Do not include private contact details, payment information, sensitive personal information, or another person's confidential information in a review or response. Reviewers may edit their own reviews where the edit option is available. Contact [support@eezevent.com](mailto:support@eezevent.com) about removal, correction, privacy, or abuse concerns. Publicly displayed content can be copied by other users, and we cannot erase copies independently retained by them.

### 4.2 With Service Providers

We use service providers to operate the Platform. As of the effective date of this Policy, production user data is processed or stored using **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. We may also use service providers for authentication, notifications, hosting, infrastructure, storage, email or communication delivery, app updates, security, and support.

These providers may process information only as needed to provide services to us and are expected to protect information appropriately.

### 4.3 Authentication and Platform Services

If you use Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, information is exchanged with Google or Apple to authenticate your account. If you enable push notifications or app updates, information may be processed through Expo or platform services needed to deliver those features.

### 4.4 Legal, Safety, and Business Transfers

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

When account deletion is completed, we immediately replace identifying profile fields with deleted-account placeholders and remove account credentials and associated sign-in and notification records. This does not mean that every related record becomes anonymous: our deletion process also retains encrypted copies of original account information in an audit vault, including name, email address, phone number, and sign-in identity information and, for vendors, business and service information. Encryption protects retained information but does not make it anonymous.

We retain records with replaced profile identifiers to preserve relationships between event, inquiry, and other workflow records. Separately, encrypted original identity records may be retained where necessary to resolve disputes or investigate fraud. Records that remain linkable to you, including through the audit vault, remain personal information; they are not treated as anonymous merely because profile fields have been replaced.

Retention is limited by the purpose of the record, whether a dispute or fraud investigation requires it, and applicable legal obligations. Preserving database relationships does not by itself require retaining original names or contact details indefinitely.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Platform, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the Platform.

The app includes account deletion flows for clients and vendor owners, and some features allow deletion of events, businesses, services, service images, catalog items, calendar blocks, team members, and inquiry attachments. Deletion may not remove all information immediately from backups, logs, legal records, audit history, transaction history, records needed to protect other users, or records we must retain for legitimate business, security, dispute-resolution, or legal reasons.

We may retain aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized information that does not identify you.

Canceling or downgrading a subscription does not itself request account deletion. Some membership and outreach records remain after a downgrade to support account history and restoration of eligible access. Account deletion and applicable retention requirements are handled separately.

Deleting the app or your EezEvent account does not itself cancel billing through Apple or Google. Use [Apple subscription settings](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions) or [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions) to stop future renewal. You may request account deletion without waiting for the subscription to expire.

Review text, ratings, Vendor responses, and related workflow records are not automatically erased by replacing your profile identifiers during account deletion. A comment or response may itself contain identifying information. Contact us to request review or response removal or correction; requests are subject to applicable privacy rights and the retention purposes described above. We do not describe retained review text or encrypted identity records as irreversibly anonymous.
