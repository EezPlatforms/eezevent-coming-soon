# EEZEVENT PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how **EezPlatforms LLC** ("EezEvent,"
"we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when
you access or use the EezEvent mobile application, website, and related services
(collectively, the "Platform").

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that we collect and use information as
described in this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, do not use the
Platform.

# 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your
use of the Platform, and limited technical information needed to operate the
Platform.

## 1.1 Account and Authentication Information

We may collect:

* Name, email address, and phone number.
* Account role, such as client, event planner, vendor owner, business team
  member, service team member, or event team member.
* Planner status and account preferences.
* Login credentials when email/password login is available.
* Information received from authentication providers, including Google Sign-In
  and Apple Sign-In, such as identity tokens and account profile information
  needed to create or authenticate your EezEvent account.
* Legal document acceptance records, including accepted policy or agreement
  version and acceptance timestamp.

## 1.2 Client, Planner, and Event Information

Clients and planners may provide:

* Event names, descriptions, event types, dates, start and end times, zip codes,
  guest counts, budgets, and requested service categories.
* Event profile images and other uploaded event materials.
* Client contact information used by planners or vendors to create or manage
  events on behalf of a client.
* Event team member details, including names, email addresses, and phone
  numbers.

## 1.3 Vendor, Business, and Service Information

Vendors and vendor team members may provide:

* Business names, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, descriptions,
  and business addresses.
* Service information, including service type, service area, pricing, service
  descriptions, social links, availability details, and related business
  information.
* Service images, main photos, catalog item images, catalog titles, catalog
  prices, catalog notes, and display order.
* Business team member and service team member details, including names, email
  addresses, and phone numbers.
* Calendar blocks, unavailable time periods, reservations, and scheduling
  details.

## 1.4 Inquiry, Messaging, Quote, Contract, and Document Information

When users interact through the Platform, we may collect:

* Vendor search, outreach, and lead information.
* Inquiry details, inquiry status, quote amounts, quote responses, contract
  status, scheduling status, cancellation status, completion status, and inquiry
  audit history.
* Messages and conversation metadata between clients, planners, vendors, and
  team members.
* Uploaded inquiry attachments, such as quotes, contracts, invoices, schedules,
  permits, receipts, and other supporting documents.
* Availability checks and overlapping commitment information used to help users
  understand scheduling conflicts.

## 1.5 Photos, Files, and Device Permissions

The mobile app may request access to your photo library so you can upload event
images, vendor service images, catalog item images, and inquiry attachments. We
collect the files you choose to upload. We do not access photos or files unless
you select them for use with the Platform.

The Android app configuration may include media and audio-related permissions.
As of the effective date of this Policy, EezEvent uses media access to support
image and file uploads and does not offer payment processing through the app.

## 1.6 Notifications and Device Information

If you enable or interact with push notifications, we may collect:

* Expo push notification token.
* Device operating system.
* Device label or device name when available.
* Notification-related metadata, such as unread message counts or inquiry
  identifiers needed to route you to the right screen.

We may also collect device and technical information such as IP address, browser
type when using web pages, device type, timestamps, app version, update channel,
runtime version, logs, diagnostics, and server request information needed to
operate, secure, troubleshoot, and update the Platform.

## 1.7 Local Storage and Session Information

The mobile app may store limited session and preference information on your
device, such as authentication token, user type, email address, phone number,
name, planner status, theme preference, and cached feature state. Website pages
may use browser storage or cookies for session or site functionality if needed.

## 1.8 Information from Other Users and Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

* Clients, planners, vendors, business team members, service team members, and
  event team members who invite you, message you, create an event involving you,
  or add your contact details to a team or workflow.
* Google and Apple when you use their sign-in services.
* Service providers that help us operate the Platform.

# 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

* Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure accounts.
* Provide client, planner, vendor, business, service, event, inquiry, messaging,
  document, calendar, outreach, lead, reservation, and notification features.
* Allow users to search vendors, send inquiries, respond to inquiries, exchange
  messages, upload documents, send quotes, manage contracts, schedule work, and
  track booking status.
* Display vendor profiles, service catalogs, event details, inquiry details,
  dashboards, notifications, and calendar views to authorized users.
* Send service-related notices, push notifications, email validation messages,
  legal-document prompts, support responses, and operational updates.
* Provide customer support and respond to requests.
* Monitor, troubleshoot, secure, maintain, and improve the Platform.
* Enforce agreements, prevent misuse, protect rights and safety, and comply with
  legal obligations.

As of the effective date of this Policy, EezEvent does **not** use analytics,
advertising, attribution, or tracking SDKs in the mobile app, and does **not**
sell personal information.

# 3. Payments

EezEvent does not process payments in the submitted app version as of the
effective date of this Policy. Quotes, contracts, reservation information, and
related documents are used for planning and workflow coordination. If payment
features are added later, we will update this Policy as appropriate.

# 4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information in the following ways:

## 4.1 With Clients, Planners, Vendors, and Team Members

Information may be shared with other users when needed to provide Platform
features. For example:

* Event details, service needs, messages, inquiries, uploaded documents, quote
  status, contract status, scheduling information, and contact information may
  be visible to users involved in the relevant event, inquiry, business,
  service, or team workflow.
* Vendor business profiles, service details, pricing, photos, catalog items,
  public contact information, and availability-related information may be shown
  to clients and planners.
* Client or planner event information may be shown to vendors when a user sends
  an inquiry, responds to outreach, or otherwise interacts with a vendor.

## 4.2 With Service Providers

We use service providers to operate the Platform. As of the effective date of
this Policy, production user data is processed or stored using **Amazon Web
Services (AWS)**. We may also use service providers for authentication,
notifications, hosting, infrastructure, storage, email or communication
delivery, app updates, security, and support.

These providers may process information only as needed to provide services to us
and are expected to protect information appropriately.

## 4.3 Authentication and Platform Services

If you use Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, information is exchanged with Google
or Apple to authenticate your account. If you enable push notifications or app
updates, information may be processed through Expo or platform services needed
to deliver those features.

## 4.4 Legal, Safety, and Business Transfers

We may disclose information if we believe disclosure is necessary to:

* Comply with law, legal process, or government requests.
* Enforce our agreements or policies.
* Protect the rights, safety, security, or property of EezEvent, users, vendors,
  or others.
* Investigate or prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, or technical issues.
* Support a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or
  similar business transaction.

# 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the
Platform, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve
disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the Platform.

The app includes account deletion flows for clients and vendor owners, and some
features allow deletion of events, businesses, services, service images, catalog
items, calendar blocks, team members, and inquiry attachments. Deletion may not
remove all information immediately from backups, logs, legal records, audit
history, transaction history, records needed to protect other users, or records
we must retain for legitimate business, security, dispute-resolution, or legal
reasons.

We may retain aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized information that does not
identify you.

# 6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards
designed to protect information. However, no system, network, or storage method
is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account
credentials and for limiting access to your device.
