Cancellation does not itself refund an amount already charged. Refund requests are handled under the applicable store's processes and mandatory legal rights. Pro features remain available while the effective Pro entitlement remains valid, including an applicable verified trial or grace period.

Downgrading does not itself delete retained membership or outreach records; account and data deletion are addressed separately in the Privacy Policy. Basic permits one active business. This restriction applies to all inquiry access, including inquiries for the business you intend to keep active. Making a business inactive does not itself delete that business or its records. Pro permits up to three owned businesses; creation of additional businesses remains subject to the applicable plan limits.
