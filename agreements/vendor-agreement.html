A change of plan, restoration, or resubscription does not guarantee another trial. Review the store confirmation for your offer, first charge, and renewal details.

6.5 Cancellation, Refunds, and Restoration

Manage or cancel through Apple subscription settings or Google Play subscriptions, using the account that made the purchase. 6.6 Plan Changes and Loss of Pro Access

Upgrade and downgrade timing, any charges or credits, and effects on an introductory offer follow the purchasing store's applicable terms. A scheduled change takes effect on its confirmed effective date. Pro features remain available while the effective Pro entitlement remains valid, including an applicable verified trial or grace period.

When a downgrade to Basic becomes effective or Pro access expires, delegated business and service team access and Pro lead discovery and outreach are restricted. Retained team memberships may become accessible again when the owner's Pro access is restored. Downgrading does not itself delete retained membership or outreach records; account and data deletion are addressed separately in the Privacy Policy. Basic permits one active business. If you downgrade from Pro with multiple active businesses, you must choose the one business to keep active and make all other businesses inactive. Once the downgrade takes effect, you cannot access any inquiries until you have reduced your active businesses to one. This restriction applies to all inquiry access, including inquiries for the business you intend to keep active. Making a business inactive does not itself delete that business or its records. Pro permits up to three owned businesses; creation of additional businesses remains subject to the applicable plan limits.
